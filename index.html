Five Stages

From warm-up to fork resolution

Each stage is a live task. You show what you understand by doing it, and the difficulty climbs as you go.

Stage 1 15 min

Sort the Chain

You get a scattered set of blocks with hashes, previous hashes, and nonces blanked out. Compute the missing values using the platform's hash calculator, match predecessors, and submit the correct chain order. Three guesses max, with a 60-second lockout per wrong attempt.

100 pts + up to 20 speed bonus
Stage 2 35-45 min

Mine to Match

You're handed a difficulty target, such as finding a nonce whose hash starts with "00", and use the hash calculator to search. Most teams split the nonce range across teammates. The backend allows one attempt every 3 seconds, so careful range-splitting across teammates pays off.

150 pts + up to 30 speed bonus
Stage 3 30 min

Catch the Forger

Two competing 6-block chains come printed on physical cards and split across your team: one teammate holds the hash fields, another the previous-hash fields, a third the raw block data. Cross-check using the hash calculator, find exactly where a stored previous-hash doesn't match, and explain in writing why that block is invalid.

130 + 20 explanation + up to 20 speed
Stage 4 Optional Bonus

Crack the Contract

A smart contract is live on Ethereum Sepolia testnet. Connect MetaMask to the challenge site, inspect the contract and its ABI through the browser console, call functions, send transactions, and reach the success state without writing any Solidity. Skipping this stage doesn't hurt your base score.

+50 flat on success · no time component
Stage 5 Finalists only

Consensus Finale

Organizers merge the mined blocks from Stage 2, across all four to six finalist teams, into one shared pool and load them onto every finalist's laptop. Different teams mined on top of the same earlier blocks, so the pool holds multiple branches. Reconstruct the valid branches, submit the longest one, and watch the leaderboard update live on the projector.

10 pts/valid branch (max 50) + 100 for longest · 20-25 min

Rulebook

What you need to know

Break one of these and your team is out. Your check-in packet holds the rest of the rulebook.

Eligibility & Teams

Open to all college students across every stream. No prior blockchain knowledge is needed, and there is no restriction on inter-college or inter-specialization teams.

Teams of 2-3 members, finalized before the event begins. No walk-in additions once the opening ceremony starts.

Register through Unstop before the event. Each team gets a unique ID used to track scores across all stages.

AI & Tools

AI assistants are allowed for general reference. Each stage still requires live, rate-limited interaction with the puzzle tools by a person at the keyboard.

Scripting, automating, or bulk-querying the hash calculator or the submission endpoints is a rules violation and can mean disqualification.

Stage-Specific

Stage 1: 3 guesses max, 60-second lockout between wrong attempts.

Stage 2: backend enforces one nonce attempt every 3 seconds per team.

Stage 4 is optional, and skipping it doesn't affect your base score.

Stage 5 is open only to the top 4-6 teams after Stage 3 plus any Stage 4 bonus.

Fair Play

Sharing block data, hashes, or answers between teams is strictly prohibited and leads to disqualification of all teams involved.

Physical cards issued in Stage 3 must stay within your assigned table or station at all times.

Teams may not interfere with, view, or attempt to access another team's session on the event platform.

Judging & Disputes

Decisions from judges and volunteers on completion, timing, and rubric scores are final.

Raise any dispute with a volunteer or judge at the relevant stage table before moving to the next stage.

What You Submit

Stage 1: submitted block order. Stage 2: nonce satisfying the difficulty target. Stage 3: identified tampered block + written explanation.

Stage 4 (bonus): successful contract exploit via transaction hash or automatic detection. Stage 5: branch reconstruction (finalists only).
